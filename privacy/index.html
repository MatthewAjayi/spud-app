Privacy policy

Your runs stay yours.

Spud is a running coach that pauses the apps you choose until your run is done. It has no account and no server. This page explains what that means for your data. Last updated 27 September 2026.

What we collect

Nothing. Spud does not collect, store or sell personal data on any server. There are no ads, no analytics SDKs and no tracking.

What stays on your iPhone

Your plan, runs, streak, badges, settings and promise signature are stored only on your device. Deleting the app, or Profile › Reset Spud, erases them.

Location

While you record a run, Spud uses GPS to measure distance and pace. Location is only used during a run you started, and the route never leaves your phone.

Apple Health

If you connect Apple Health, Spud reads running workouts to verify your training and writes the runs you record in the app. Health data is never used for advertising, never shared with third parties and never leaves your device through Spud. You control access in Settings › Health.

Screen Time

Apple's Screen Time framework pauses the apps you pick. Apple keeps that selection private: Spud never learns which apps they are.

Game Center

If you sign in to Game Center, your Game Center nickname and scores from verified runs appear on leaderboards your friends can see. Apple operates Game Center under its own privacy policy.

Strava and Fitbit

If you connect either service, Spud downloads your runs directly from it to your phone over an encrypted connection and keeps the sign-in in the iPhone keychain. Disconnecting removes it.

Purchases

Spud Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple handles payment; Spud never sees your payment details. Manage or cancel in Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions.

Children

Spud is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Contact

Questions about privacy: send us a request. If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
